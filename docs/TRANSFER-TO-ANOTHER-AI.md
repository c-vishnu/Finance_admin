## 3. Non-negotiable accounting safeguards

1. Never create or persist an unbalanced journal.
2. Normal operational workflows must derive accounts automatically; do not expose debit/credit selection to ordinary users.
3. Sales and purchase orders do not post accounting. Their converted documents remain drafts until explicitly posted.
4. Posted history is corrected through reversal, credit/debit note, or adjustment—not deletion or silent mutation.
5. Preserve account codes and journal references. Do not convert demonstration balances into opening journals.
6. Official reports must use posted accounting facts and distinguish unposted documents.
7. Company, branch, period, tax, customer, vendor, item, and account mappings must remain traceable.
8. Browser role selectors and UI gates are demonstrations, not production authorization.

## 9. Known limitations

- This remains a frontend/browser prototype, not a production multi-tenant accounting service.
- Browser storage is not transactional, tamper-proof, backed up, or shared between devices/accounts.
- UI permission labels do not provide authenticated authorization.
- Statutory, security, concurrency, disaster-recovery, and database guarantees remain future backend work.
- The production bundle currently exceeds Vite's advisory size threshold and should eventually be split by route/module.
- The legacy stylesheet `src/styles.css` still carries app-bar element rules (`header{position:fixed;height:58px}`, `header label{...}`, `header>button{...}`, and `@media(max-width:1050px){header>button:not(.primary):not(.hamb){display:none}}`) that leak into every `<header>` in the app. Components that portal a drawer or modal header must reset them, and a plain control row is safer as a `div`. These rules are the root cause of the hidden close buttons and the forced 38px filter labels; a future cleanup should scope them to the application top bar.
