# Cash Flow Statement

**Last verified:** 2026-09-28

> **Report toolbar update — 2026-09-29:** the heading action row now carries a date-range preset dropdown (`All dates`, `Today`, `This week`, `This month`, `This year`, `Custom selection`) beside the search box and the `Filters` disclosure, and ONE `Export` button whose dropdown holds the report own exports. Where a sentence below still describes `Export Excel` and `Export PDF` as two side-by-side buttons, the dropdown supersedes it. See `docs/REPORT-TOOLBAR.md`.

`src/CashFlowStatement.jsx` is a read-only, period-based statement of the cash and cash-equivalent movements that actually happened, and `src/cash-flow.js` is its pure projection. `cashFlow(state,{from,to,branch,cashAccount,account,accountGroup,search,includeZero})` walks the shared account store, keeps only journals that are posted (`!journal.status||journal.status==='Posted'`), and reads nothing from the invoice, purchase, receipt or expense records - only the posted ledger. It is never `revenue - expenses`: a credit sale or credit purchase moves no money and creates no cash flow until it is settled, at which point only the settlement's cash leg is stated. Draft and cancelled documents never reach it (an unposted document carries no journal lines), while a posted reversal is an ordinary entry, so a reversed pair nets to zero by itself.

Cash and cash equivalents are the accounts the Chart of Accounts already classifies as money, read through `isMoneyAccount` (re-exported here as `isCashEquivalent`) - the same rule `src/simple-journal-transaction.js` uses to offer a money account to the transaction form, so the report, the form and the banking module can never disagree about which accounts are money. No account code is named anywhere in this module, so a chart that renames or re-codes its cash and bank accounts keeps working.

Each cash leg is classified by what the other side of its own entry was, in a fixed order: Equity (Drawings, Capital, else other financing), Loans, Fixed Assets, an investment read from the account wording, GST Payable / Tax Liability, Income (Other Income falls to other operating, otherwise customers), Accounts Receivable, Expenses (Cost of Goods Sold to suppliers, else operating expenses), Accounts Payable, Inventory, Other Assets (other investing), a customer-facing Liability, and finally the catch-all other operating line. The rules read the existing account metadata (`type`, `accountNature`, `group`, name) rather than a document label, and every movement lands on exactly one line, so one account may appear under two lines - Bank received from customers and paid suppliers - without ever being counted twice.

`ACTIVITY_SECTIONS` is the fixed vocabulary the statement is stated in: Operating Activities (Cash received from customers, Cash paid to suppliers, Cash paid for operating expenses, Taxes paid, Other operating cash flows), Investing Activities (Purchase of fixed assets, Sale of fixed assets, Purchase/sale of investments, Other investing cash flows) and Financing Activities (Capital introduced, Loans received, Loan repayments, Owner drawings / distributions, Other financing cash flows), each closing on its own "Net Cash from ... Activities" total. An empty line is hidden with its rows unless the zero-balance toggle is on, and that toggle never changes a line or section total.

A journal that only moves money between two of the business's own cash or bank accounts changes the mix of cash, never the amount of it, so it is not invented as an inflow and an outflow: it is stated once on the derived `internalTransfers` row ("Transfers between cash and bank accounts") and sits outside the three activities. `openingCash` is the ledger balance of the selected accounts strictly before the From date and `closingCash` is their balance at the To date, so `openingCash + operating + investing + financing + internalTransfers + excluded === closingCash` holds by construction. `difference` is reported and never forced to zero, the ten `report.checks` re-derive every total from the rows it claims to summarise, and `report.valid` is their conjunction; the page states the result instead of assuming the arithmetic held.

Account, Account group and Search narrow which movements are classified, and the movements they leave out are stated on the derived `excluded` row ("Cash movements excluded by the current filters") rather than silently unbalancing the statement, which is why closing cash still agrees with the ledger under any filter. The account and account-group filters read the accounts on the other side of the entry - the options the page offers are the non-cash accounts - while the free-text search reads every account in the entry, the cash or bank account included, so searching a cash account finds that account's own movements instead of blanking the report. An entry whose counterpart account is not in the chart is still stated, on the catch-all operating line. Branch filtering uses the same rule as the Balance Sheet, Trial Balance and Profit & Loss, and `report.branchOptions` is derived from the branch identities the loaded journal actually carries instead of a hard-coded list.

`report.statement` is one ordered list - opening cash, the three activity sections in order, the transfers row when it is non-zero, the excluded row when it is non-zero, Net Increase / (Decrease) in Cash, opening cash again, and closing cash - that both the page and `cashFlowExportRows` walk, so the sheet can never drift from the screen. Money leaves the engine in integer paise, as every other report does, and is stated in rupees in the sheet; the export carries a blank separator row before its column header, as `profitLossExportRows` does. `report.periodActivity` records whether the period had any cash movement before the row filters narrowed it, which is how the page tells "nothing was posted this period" apart from "nothing matches these filters". `financialYearStart(date)` is re-exported from `src/profit-loss.js` and returns the 1 April the rest of the product opens its financial year on.

The page reuses the report shell already established by the Balance Sheet, the Trial Balance and the Profit & Loss: one white report surface, a quiet heading with the title and the description "Track cash inflows and outflows for the selected period.", an inline account search, an anchored Filters popover, Export Excel and Export PDF. Five summary cards state Opening Cash, Cash Inflows, Cash Outflows, Net Cash Flow and Closing Cash, and the statement caption repeats the period. Filters own From date, To date, Branch, Cash / Bank account, Account, Account group and the zero-balance toggle, with every option derived from the loaded chart rather than hard-coded, and the search is applied through `useDeferredValue` so the page reports `aria-busy` while recalculating. An account row hands off to the General Ledger by writing `sessionStorage['wayvida-open-account']` and navigating, the same handoff the Balance Sheet and the Trial Balance use. A sticky footer reads "Cash Flow Statement reconciles" with the closing cash, or states the actual difference on a genuine mismatch, and the empty state keeps opening and closing cash visible. The page reads the account store and never writes it, and never posts, edits or reverses an entry.

Three shell rules bite here and were only visible in the running app: the report heading is a `<header>` element, so `.cfHead` restates `position:static!important;inset:auto!important;z-index:auto!important;height:auto!important` exactly as `.bsHead`, `.tbHead` and `.plHead` do, or the bare `header{position:fixed;...}` in `src/styles.css` lifts it out of the flow and over the summary cards; `th:last-child,td:last-child{...text-align:right}` from `src/ui-system.css` right-aligns the single-cell section and group rows unless `.cfStatementCard .cfSection th`/`.cfGroup th` restate `text-align:left!important`; and every subtotal keeps its label and amount in ONE full-width `.cfRowLine` cell, because below 560px the hidden Account Code and Account Group cells would otherwise leave a three-column label span with a zero-width amount cell and print every total without its amount.
