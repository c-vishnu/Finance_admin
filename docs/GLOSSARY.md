# Glossary

**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

- **Account:** Ledger classification used by journal lines.
- **Accounts Receivable (AR):** Asset representing amounts customers owe.
- **Accounts Payable (AP):** Liability representing amounts owed to vendors.
- **Allocation:** Application of a receipt or credit to an invoice; not necessarily a new cash posting.
- **Cess:** Additional tax component applied where configured.
- **CGST / SGST:** Central and state GST components for intra-state supply.
- **Control account:** Account posted by operational modules rather than ordinary manual journals.
- **Cost centre:** Management reporting dimension.
- **Credit note:** Customer credit that reverses part or all of revenue/tax/receivable effects.
- **Day Book:** Chronological read-only listing of posted vouchers; unposted review is separated.
- **Debit / Credit:** The two sides of double-entry accounting; neither inherently means positive or negative.
- **Financial period:** Date range whose posting state may be Open, Closing, Soft locked, Locked or Reopened.
- **GSTIN:** Indian Goods and Services Tax registration identifier.
- **Hard lock:** Period state blocking backdated accounting mutations until controlled reopening.
- **IGST:** Integrated GST for inter-state supply.
- **Idempotency key:** Token ensuring a command is applied once.
- **Journal:** Balanced collection of debit/credit lines representing an accounting event.
- **Ledger:** Account-wise projection of posted journal lines and running balance.
- **Normal balance:** Expected debit or credit nature of an account type.
- **Opening balance:** Balance brought into the system, posted through a balanced opening journal.
- **Posted:** Accounting state indicating the journal has been created and included in ledgers/reports.
- **Reversal:** New journal with opposite lines that preserves the original record.
- **Sales order:** Non-accounting commercial document that may generate an invoice draft.
- **Soft lock:** Closed period allowing only authorized, warned/approved exceptions.
- **TDS:** Indian tax deducted at source.
- **Trial Balance:** Account balances proving total debits equal total credits.
