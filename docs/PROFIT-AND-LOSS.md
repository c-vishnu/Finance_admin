# Profit & Loss

**Last verified:** 2026-09-28

> **Report toolbar update — 2026-09-29:** the heading action row now carries a date-range preset dropdown (`All dates`, `Today`, `This week`, `This month`, `This year`, `Custom selection`) beside the search box and the `Filters` disclosure, and ONE `Export` button whose dropdown holds the report own exports. Where a sentence below still describes `Export Excel` and `Export PDF` as two side-by-side buttons, the dropdown supersedes it. See `docs/REPORT-TOOLBAR.md`.

`src/ProfitLoss.jsx` is a read-only, period-based statement of income and expenses, and `src/profit-loss.js` is its pure projection. `profitAndLoss(state,{from,to,branch,account,accountType,accountGroup,search,includeZero})` walks the shared account store, keeps only journals that are posted (`!journal.status||journal.status==='Posted'`) and dated inside the selected window (`from <= journal.date <= to`), and reads nothing from the invoice, purchase, receipt or expense records - only the posted ledger. Drafts never reach it: an unposted document is not an accounting transaction and carries no journal lines, so there is nothing to project. Cancelled source documents are outside the posted journal, while a posted reversal is included as an entry in its own right, which is what makes a reversed pair net to zero and a posted credit note reduce revenue exactly once.

Income is stated credit-positive and Expenses debit-positive, which is the side each type is expected on; a contra movement is reported as the negative it is rather than being forced positive, so the statement can never flatter the result. `PROFIT_LOSS_TYPES` is `['Income','Expenses']`, because assets, liabilities and equity are balance-sheet accounts and are never stated here. Section, group and account hierarchy comes from the Chart of Accounts itself through `normalizeAccounts`, `account.group` and `accountGroupFor` - there is no second account taxonomy in this module. Group accounts (`isGroup`) and accounts outside the two reportable types are excluded, and zero-balance accounts are hidden unless the Include zero-balance toggle is on; that toggle is a display filter and never touches the account records or a section total.

The statement is five sections in a fixed order - `revenue` (Income outside `Other Income`), `cogs` (Expenses in `Cost of Goods Sold`), `operatingExpenses` (Expenses outside COGS and Other Expenses), `otherIncome` (`Other Income`) and `otherExpenses` (`Other Expenses`) - each carrying the groups the chart assigned it, known groups first through `REVENUE_GROUP_ORDER` and `EXPENSE_GROUP_ORDER` and any custom group the chart defines alphabetically after them. Empty sections are omitted. The derived rows are the accounting arithmetic itself: `grossProfit = totalRevenue - totalCogs`, `operatingProfit = grossProfit - totalOperatingExpenses`, `netProfit = operatingProfit + totalOtherIncome - totalOtherExpenses`, `totalExpenses = totalCogs + totalOperatingExpenses + totalOtherExpenses`, and `margin = totalRevenue > 0 ? netProfit/totalRevenue*100 : null`, which the page prints as `N/A` rather than dividing by zero. `loss` is `netProfit < 0` and drives the "Net Loss" label, so a negative result is never printed as a Net Profit. Money leaves the engine in integer paise, as every other report does.

`Other Income` is treated as non-operating: it is excluded from Total Revenue and added after Operating Profit, exactly as the required subtotal order states. That is a deliberate accounting decision rather than a defect - a chart that wants interest income inside revenue should move the account out of the `Other Income` group.

`report.statement` is one ordered list of section and derived rows that both the page and `profitLossExportRows` walk, so the sheet can never drift from the screen. `report.checks` re-derives every total from the rows it claims to summarise and asserts that no account is counted twice; `report.valid` is the conjunction of those ten checks and the page states the result instead of assuming the arithmetic held. `report.periodActivity` records whether the period had any non-zero income or expense movement before row filters narrowed the rows, which is how the page tells "nothing was posted this period" apart from "nothing matches these filters". `financialYearStart(date)` returns the 1 April the rest of the product opens its financial year on (April to March, the same convention as `src/budget-store.js`), and the page defaults to that date through today.

A section whose only group carries the section's own name is marked `collapsed` and states that heading once: the chart has no finer taxonomy than the group, so `Operating Expenses` and `Cost of Goods Sold` would otherwise head the same block twice and repeat the section total as a group total. Nothing is hidden - the group name still prints in the Account Group column of every row, still filters, and still reconciles.

The page reuses the report shell already established by the Balance Sheet, the Trial Balance and the Day Book: one white report surface, a quiet heading with the title and the description "View income, expenses and net profit for the selected period.", an inline account search, an anchored Filters popover, Export Excel and Export PDF. Four summary cards state Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Net Profit or Net Loss and Profit Margin, and the statement caption repeats the period ("Income and expenses from X to Y"). Filters own From date, To date, Branch, Account type, Account group, Account and the zero-balance toggle, and the account and group options are derived from the loaded chart rather than hard-coded. Search matches account code, account name, type, nature and group, and is applied through `useDeferredValue` so the page reports `aria-busy` while recalculating instead of freezing. An account row writes `sessionStorage['wayvida-open-account']` and navigates to the General Ledger, which is the same handoff the Balance Sheet and the Trial Balance use. The page reads the account store and never writes it, never posts, edits or reverses an entry, and deliberately renders no report identity card: the organisation / title / basis block the Balance Sheet still shows above its statement repeats the heading and pushes the statement a card further down the page, and it was removed from the Trial Balance on 2026-09-28 for the same reason.

Accounting basis is deliberately not a filter on this report. The posted ledger is single-basis (accrual) - the engine has no cash-basis posting to project - and the Balance Sheet's "Report basis" select is decorative, so a second control here would be a visual-only filter. The basis is stated as Accrual in the print and export metadata, exactly as the Trial Balance states it.

Three shell interactions are worth preserving. First, the page heading is a `<header>` element and `src/styles.css` still carries a bare `header{position:fixed;z-index:10;top:0;left:206px;right:0;height:58px;...}` rule, so `.plHead` restates `position:static!important;inset:auto!important;z-index:auto!important;height:auto!important` exactly as `.bsHead` and `.tbHead` do, because without it the heading is lifted out of the flow and overlaps the summary cards. Second, `th:last-child,td:last-child{...text-align:right}` right-aligns the section and group heading rows, whose only cell spans the whole row, so `.plStatementCard .plSection th,.plStatementCard .plGroup th` opts back to `text-align:left!important`. Third, and new here: below 700px the Account Group column and below 560px the Account Code column are `display:none`, which makes the surviving header cells fall into the first two table columns while a subtotal row's label cell still spans three - so the amount landed in a zero-width column, every subtotal printed without its amount and the card scrolled sideways to reach it. Each subtotal now states its label and amount inside one full-width cell through `.plRowLine` (a flex pair, `space-between`), which keeps the amount on the table's right edge at every width: it measures 1191.2px on the 1280px layout, identical to the money column of an account row. All three defects were invisible to the bundler and only appeared in the running app.

Below 900px the summary cards fold to two columns and the toolbar wraps; below 560px the cards fold to one column, the filter popover becomes a static panel, the two narrow statement columns are hidden and the page itself never scrolls sideways (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth === clientWidth` at 390px). If no income or expense account survives the selection the table states "No income or expense transactions were posted for this period." - or "No accounts match the current filters." when the period did have movement - with a Reset filters action, and the statement rows are replaced rather than printed as zero rows beside it. The sticky validation footer reads "Profit & Loss reconciles", or "Profit & Loss reconciles · the period is at a loss" beside the net result, or "Profit & Loss does not reconcile" on a genuine arithmetic failure. The footer note reads "Based on posted accounting entries for the selected period. Draft and cancelled transactions are excluded." Printing neutralises the report portal and prints the header metadata, the full statement and the validation line.
